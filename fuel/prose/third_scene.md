# Prose mode: third_scene

Third-person limited (or lightly omniscient) with scene-first rhythm.
Interiority is sparse and earned. Cinematography, blocking, gesture, and
dialogue do most of the work.

Use for plot-forward thrillers, epic ensemble chapters, farce, and books that
should read "wider" than one continuous head.

## Required

- Establish place, bodies, and stakes before private thought.
- Prefer action, glances, and props over reported feelings.
- When thought appears, keep it short and in the POV's register.
- Hard cuts and white space are fine; long interior monologues are not default.
- Dialogue tags and beats that advance situation, not mood essays.

## Forbidden

- Chapter-long stream-of-consciousness dumps.
- Emotion summary lines ("a sense of dread washed over them").
- Staccato fragment montages substituting for scene work.
- Explaining a joke or beat after it lands.

## Distance

Medium to wide. The room matters as much as the skull.
