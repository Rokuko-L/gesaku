# Prose mode: first_intimate

Close first-person stream-of-consciousness. The reader eavesdrops on the
POV character's live processing — unfiltered, continuous, physically anchored.

Use when the book should feel like being inside someone's head (thriller
interiority, intimate YA, certain light-novel registers). Not a license for
fragmented "punch" lines.

## Required

- Real-time access to consciousness. Physical mechanism + half-formed thought
  as events happen — not diary summaries after the fact.
- Untagged internal dialogue: questions, sarcasm, wrong guesses drop straight
  into narration without "I thought" / "I wondered."
- Fluid, grammatically complete sentences even in panic. Comma splices and
  long compound rushes are fine; staccato fragments are not.
- Each internal state gets a fresh physical anchor. No stock metaphor reused
  across scenes ("a stone in my chest," "copper and honey").
- Wrong theories and mid-scene revisions are welcome.

## Forbidden

- "I felt a wave of…" / "the realization settled in my stomach" (empty labels).
- "Run. Need to run. Can't stay here." stacks (1–4 word paragraph piles used
  as fake intensity).
- Repeated one-beat closer lines: "I don't know." / "I nod." / "I don't answer."
- After-the-fact summaries: "I realized I had been wrong about everything."

## Distance

Extreme close. Scene serves interiority; cut to sensation and inference, not
cinematography.
