# Prose mode: third_close

Close third-person limited. One POV head per chapter/scene. Thought bleeds
into narration without tags, but the grammar stays third-person.

The default for most commercial SF/F, thriller, and romance. Safer against
the staccato LLM tick than extreme first-person close PoC.

## Required

- Stay inside one character's perception per scene (sensory + knowledge).
- Free indirect style: "He was late, damn it, and the train knew it."
- Concrete action and image carry emotion; interiority clarifies, not replaces.
- Vary sentence length; prefer complete clauses over fragment stacks.
- New physical anchors for repeated emotional states.

## Forbidden

- Head-hopping mid-scene.
- "She felt a wave of…" / omniscient stage whispers from outside the POV.
- Paragraphs of 1–4 word lines used as "intensity."
- Stock metaphors repeated across chapters for the same feeling.

## Distance

Close, not fused. We ride with the POV character; we are not the raw
first-person "I" and not a distant camera.
